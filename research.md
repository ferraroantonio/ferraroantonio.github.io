<h2>Job Market Paper</h2>
<div class="card">
<strong>Inherited Housing and Household Investment: Evidence from Germany and Spain</strong> (working paper, 2026). 
<div class="meta">bequests; household assets; wealth; mortgages; investment decisions.</div>
<p>
  How do households use inherited housing? Using longitudinal household
  survey data from Germany and Spain, this paper finds evidence of
  different investment responses: households in Germany use inherited
  property as collateral for further property purchases, while those
  in Spain derive persistent rental income. The findings highlight how
  housing-market conditions shape wealth accumulation after inheritance.
</p>

    <strong>From Private Wealth to Patient Capital: Family Offices in Entrepreneurial Finance</strong>
    (with Emma Li and Haoyong Zhou), 2026.
  <p>
  This paper compares how family offices and independent venture
  capitalists invest in entrepreneurial firms. Using an original
  dataset of around 3,400 family-office-backed investments, we find
  that family offices favour larger investor syndicates, smaller
  funding rounds and investments aligned with environmental, social
  and governance goals. We also examine differences in firms'
  subsequent stock-market listings.
  </p>
